# HostSync — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 9 November 2026_

HostSync ("the app") is made by Delpier Labs ("we"). It brings the bookings of a short-term rental host from Airbnb, Booking.com, VRBO and other platforms together in one calendar, using each platform's iCal calendar link. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. Contact: tmm.dev.86@gmail.com.

## Data the app keeps on your device

The iCal links you add, the bookings imported from those calendars (dates, platform and the text the platform puts in the calendar), guest details you type in yourself (name, phone, email, number of guests), your notes, the log of messages you send and your settings are saved **only on your device**. There is no account or sign-in and we have no servers: none of this data is sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

## Calendar downloads

To show your bookings, the app downloads the iCal calendars directly from the links you provide (for example from Airbnb or Booking.com). Those services receive the request like any other access to that link, including your IP address. The app syncs when you open it and, in the Pro version, from time to time in the background.

## Messages to guests

When you send a message by WhatsApp, SMS or email, HostSync only opens that app with the text filled in. The message is sent by that app, under its own terms. HostSync keeps a copy of the text on your device so you can see what you sent.

## Notifications

Check-in, check-out and overbooking alerts are created and shown on your device. No notification service outside your phone is used.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a banner ad served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. The app asks Google only for non-personalised ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form before ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in **Calendars → Ad privacy options**. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. The Pro version shows no ads.

How Google uses this data: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>

## Purchases (Google Play)

HostSync Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play. We do not see your payment details. Google Play tells the app only whether the purchase was made, so it can unlock Pro and restore it on a new device.

Google Play privacy policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>

## Children

The app is meant for adult hosts and is not directed to children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
