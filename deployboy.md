# DeployBoy — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 30 October 2026_

DeployBoy ("the game") is made by Delpier Labs ("we"). This policy explains what data the game uses and why. Contact: tmm.dev.86@gmail.com.

## Data the game keeps on your device

High scores, settings, unlocked items and whether you bought Remove Ads are saved **on your device**. There is no account or sign-in. Only the scores you choose to share on the world leaderboard leave the device (see below).

## World leaderboard (Supabase)

When you enter your initials after a run, the game sends them to the world TOP 10 together with the score, the day reached, the game mode and a random number created by the game on its first launch. This random number is not linked to your name, account or advertising ID; it is only used to limit how often one phone can send scores. Your IP address is seen by the server when the score is sent.

The scores are stored with Supabase, in a data centre in the European Union, and the initials, scores and days are shown to every player. Scores are not sent if you do not enter initials. To have your scores removed, write to the contact address above with the date of the run.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob:

- an optional rewarded video after a game over ("WATCH AD TO ROLL BACK");
- a full-screen ad between days, at most once every two days.

To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in **Settings → Privacy options**. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

How Google uses this data: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>

## Purchases (Google Play)

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play. We do not see your payment details. Google Play tells the game only whether the purchase was made, so it can remove the ads and restore the purchase on a new device.

Google Play privacy policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>

## Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and ads are limited to content rated for teens.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
